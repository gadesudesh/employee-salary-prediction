Prediction Interval Methodology

How SalaryAI Pro computes confidence intervals for regression

Statistical Grounding

Standard linear regression produces a single point prediction without variance boundaries. In enterprise compensation decisions, knowing the confidence interval is critical for negotiating salary bands and benchmarking fair offers.

Because our tuned Random Forest regressor comprises 200 individual decision trees, each tree is trained on a distinct bootstrap sample of employee records. When candidate inputs are submitted:

  • Every one of the 200 trees calculates its independent point valuation.
  • The reported valuation is the ensemble mean: μ = (1/N) Σ tree_pred_i.
  • The 90% Confidence Interval represents the empirical 5th and 95th percentiles of all 200 tree projections, capturing model variance and data sparsity in specialized bands.

About SalaryAI Pro

Comprehensive enterprise documentation: project purpose, dataset specifications, model architecture, limitations, sample profiles, and hosting recommendations.

1. Executive Purpose & Business Value

Addressing enterprise compensation equity and market competitiveness

In competitive talent markets, salary bands frequently suffer from inconsistent negotiation outcomes, demographic pay gaps, and stale historical benchmarks. SalaryAI Pro addresses this challenge by providing an objective, data-driven machine learning valuation platform designed for:

Pay Equity Auditing Ensure candidates with comparable tenure and credentials receive fair, non-discriminatory compensation.
Talent Acquisition Offers Calibrate offer letters against verified market rates with 90% confidence bands to reduce offer rejection rates.
Career Band Planning Empower employees and engineering managers to visualize multi-year career progression and degree premiums.

3. Limitations & Ethical Guardrails

Known boundaries for responsible AI deployment

Excludes Equity & Discretionary Bonuses

The model estimates base annual cost-to-company (CTC). Non-liquid components like stock options (ESOPs), RSUs, and variable annual bonuses are not reflected.

Geographic Market Scope

Calibrated against standard corporate compensation in India. While USD equivalents are provided for reference, regional cost-of-living adjustments outside India require local multiplier tuning.

Macroeconomic Inflation Drift

Model weights reflect current market conditions. We recommend retraining the Random Forest pipeline semi-annually to account for tech market shifts and inflation.

4. Hosting, Reliability & Scalability Architecture

Zero-latency predictions and production hosting recommendations

To deliver enterprise performance, the backend is engineered with in-memory caching and startup pre-warming:

In-Memory Pre-Warming salary_model.pkl and EDA statistics are loaded once on boot and warmed with a synthetic inference pass, reducing API response times to < 15ms.
Static Cache Headers Static assets (CSS, JS, icons) are served with Cache-Control: public, max-age=86400, ensuring immediate subsequent page renders.
Upgrading Beyond Render Free Tier Free-tier cloud hosts (such as Render Free) spin down after 15 minutes of inactivity, resulting in a 50-second cold-start. Upgrading to Render Starter ($7/mo) or AWS App Runner / Cloud Run ensures persistent memory and zero cold-start delay.
